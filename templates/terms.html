Terms of Service

Yeh ek standard template hai. Launch se pehle ise kisi lawyer se review karwa lo.

1. Service kya hai

GreenBEE ek platform hai jo customers ko verified companions se milata hai. Sirf platonic, public-place companionship (baat-cheet, ghoomna, events me saath dena jaise kaam). Koi bhi sexual ya illegal activity bilkul allowed nahi hai.

2. Eligibility

Customer aur companion dono ki umar kam se kam 18 saal honi chahiye. Galat jaankari dene par account band kiya ja sakta hai.

3. Bookings

Booking ek request hoti hai — companion accept kare tab hi confirm hoti hai. Jo request start time tak accept na ho wo automatically cancel ho jati hai. Payment customer aur companion ke beech meeting par seedha hota hai (platform abhi online payment nahi leta). Platform ka commission companion ke payout se adjust hota hai.

4. Safety

Hamesha public jagah par milo. Kuch bhi galat lage to dashboard se booking par Report ya SOS use karo, aur emergency me turant 112 par call karo. SOS ek alert hai — ye police/ambulance ki jagah nahi leta.

5. Prohibited behaviour

Harassment, dhamki, no-show, fake profile, platform ke bahar paise ya personal data ki maang, aur kisi bhi illegal kaam par account suspend/blacklist ho sakta hai aur zarurat par authorities ko jaankari di ja sakti hai.

6. Verification

Companion ka profile admin verification ke baad hi live hota hai. Verification kisi bhi tarah ki guarantee nahi hai; apni suraksha ka dhyan khud bhi rakho.

7. Liability

GreenBEE sirf ek platform hai. Law ke dayre me rehte hue, users ke beech hui meeting se hone wale nuksan ke liye hamari zimmedari seemit hai.
