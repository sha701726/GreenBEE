Privacy Policy

1. Hum kaun sa data lete hain

Sabhi users: Google account ka naam aur email, phone number, city aur aapki location (latitude/longitude). Companions: date of birth, gender, photo, bio, languages/skills, hourly rate, ID proof ka type aur number (encrypted form me store hota hai), emergency contact. Bookings: date, time, amount, rating/review, reports.

2. Data ka use

Aas-paas ke companions dikhane, bookings manage karne, companions ko verify karne, safety (reports/SOS) handle karne aur service sudharne ke liye. Hum aapka data bechte nahi hain.

3. Kaun dekh sakta hai

Booking confirm hone par customer aur companion ek-doosre ka phone number dekh sakte hain. Companion ki exact location, ID proof aur emergency contact public nahi hote; ID proof sirf verification ke liye admin team dekhti hai. Safety ke case me ya kanoon ke mutabiq authorities ke saath data share kiya ja sakta hai.

4. Location

Location sirf nearby companions dikhane aur bookings ke liye use hoti hai. Browser permission ke bina service use nahi ho sakti.

5. Aapke adhikaar

Aap dashboard ke Profile tab me apni details edit kar sakte ho, account deactivate kar sakte ho, ya "Delete my data" se apni personal information hata sakte ho (booking history anonymous form me rehti hai). Active bookings hone par pehle unhe cancel/complete karna hoga.

6. Security aur retention

ID proof numbers encrypted store hote hain. Account delete karne ya legal zarurat khatam hone par data hata diya jata hai.

7. 18+ only

Service sirf 18 saal ya usse upar ke logon ke liye hai.
